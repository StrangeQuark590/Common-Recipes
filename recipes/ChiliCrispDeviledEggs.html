Chili Crisp Deviled Eggs

These chili crisp deviled eggs are creamy deviled eggs that get a bold, spicy upgrade

with chili crisp, tangy rice vinegar, and a hint of mustard. Finished with a drizzle of

extra chili crisp and fresh green onions, these bite-sized appetizers are rich, flavorful,

and just a little fiery—perfect for gatherings or snacking. I used Lao Gan Ma Spicy Chili

Crisp, but feel free to use your favorite brand.

Ingredients

  • 6 large hard-boiled eggs
  • 3 tablespoons mayonnaise
  • 2 1/2 tablespoons chili crisp, such as Lao Gan Ma Spicy Chili Crisp®, divided
  • 1 teaspoon seasoned rice vinegar
  • 1/4 teaspoon dry mustard
  • salt to taste
  • 1 green onion, chopped

Steps

  1. Halve eggs lengthwise and scoop egg yolks into a bowl; set egg whites aside. Mash yolks with a fork.
  2. Add mayonnaise, 1 teaspoon of chili crisp, rice vinegar, dry mustard, and salt to the egg yolks; combine well until smooth.
  3. Place yolk mixture into a small resealable bag and snip off a corner. Pipe mixture into egg whites. Top with remaining chili crisp and green onions. Refrigerate until ready to serve.
